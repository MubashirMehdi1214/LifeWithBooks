Cookie Policy

What cookies are, why we use them, and how you can take control.

Last updated: 28 May 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device when you visit it. They help the site remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language and other settings, and they help us understand how visitors use the site.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for the basic functions of the website, such as remembering your menu state on mobile.
  • Analytics cookies — used by services such as Google Analytics to collect anonymous statistics on page views, time on page and traffic sources. These cookies help us improve the site.
  • Advertising cookies — if we display ads through Google AdSense or similar networks, these cookies may be used to show ads that are relevant to your interests and to limit how many times you see the same ad.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

If ads appear on this site, Google AdSense and its advertising partners may use cookies and identifiers to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can learn more and opt out of personalised ads at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and aboutads.info.

How to Control Cookies

You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • Block all cookies.
  • Block third-party cookies only.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Be notified when a new cookie is set.

For step-by-step instructions, see the documentation for your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
