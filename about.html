About LifeWithBooks

A free, friendly home for language learners and curious readers everywhere.

Hello, and welcome. LifeWithBooks started as a simple idea: language and learning should never be locked behind a paywall. Whether you are a student preparing for an exam, a parent helping a child learn a new language, or an adult finally chasing the language you always wanted to speak — this library is built for you.

We are a small editorial team that hand-picks each title before it is added to the catalogue. We read the description, check the level, and write a short, honest summary in plain English. No filler content, no clickbait, no fake reviews. Just useful books you can actually learn from.

Our Mission

Make high-quality educational reading material easy to discover and free to access for self-learners around the world. We believe a curious reader in Karachi, Lagos, Manila or Bogotá deserves the same quality of learning material as a student at a private university.

What You’ll Find Here

  • Language learning books — English, French, German, Spanish, Deutsch and more, sorted by level.
  • Grammar & vocabulary — practical workbooks, reference grammars and word-building books.
  • Kids’ education — phonics, reading comprehension, writing prompts and early-grade resources.
  • Self-development & business — practical guides on communication, leadership and personal growth.
  • Original guides — learning tips and reading recommendations written by our editors.

How We Choose Books

Every title goes through three simple checks before it appears on the site:

  1. Educational value — does this book genuinely help the learner it is aimed at?
  2. Clarity — is the writing clear, well organised and free of obvious errors?
  3. Public availability — the file should already be publicly available online; we link, we do not host.

Meet the Editor

LifeWithBooks is curated by Mubashir Mehdi, an avid reader and language-learning enthusiast based in Karachi, Pakistan. After spending years collecting useful learning resources from across the web, he started LifeWithBooks to share that collection in an organised, easy-to-browse format. You can reach him directly on the contact page.

Respect for Authors and Publishers

We deeply respect the work of authors and publishers. If you are a rights holder and you would like a book removed from our catalogue, please follow the simple process on our DMCA / Copyright page. We respond to every valid request within three working days.

If a book really helps you, we always encourage purchasing a copy from the original publisher to support the people who created it.
