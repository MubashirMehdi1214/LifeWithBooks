Our Mission

LifeWithBooks was founded on one simple belief: great books should be accessible to everyone, everywhere, regardless of income or location.

We curate and create free PDF books covering classic literature, language learning, personal development, and educational guides — all completely free, no signup required.

Our readers come from over 50 countries. Whether you are a student in Karachi, a professional in London, a parent in Toronto, or a reader in Lagos — LifeWithBooks is for you.

What You’ll Find Here

  • Classic literature — Pride and Prejudice, Sherlock Holmes, Dracula, and dozens of public-domain masterpieces.
  • English learning — grammar, vocabulary, conversation practice and exam preparation material.
  • Language learning — French, German, Spanish, Deutsch and more.
  • Self-development — timeless guides on mindset, success and personal growth.
  • Kids and young readers — fables, fairy tales and early learning resources.
  • Science, technology and wellness — programming guides and natural health PDFs.

How We Choose Books

Every title goes through three simple checks before it appears on the site:

  1. Educational value — does this book genuinely help the reader it is aimed at?
  2. Clarity — is the writing clear, well organised and useful?
  3. Legal availability — public-domain classics and original guides we create; we respect all copyrights.
