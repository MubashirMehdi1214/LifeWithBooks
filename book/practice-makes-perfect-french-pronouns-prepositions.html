Original LifeWithBooks guide — Dedicated practice on two of the trickiest areas of French grammar — pronouns (y, en, le, la, les, lui, leur, dont, etc. ) and prepositions (à, de, en, dans, pour, par, etc. ). French pronouns and prepositions are notoriously confusing for learners. The pronoun system alone includes subject, direct object, indirect object, reflexive, relative, demonstrative and possessive pronouns — plus the tricky 'y' and 'en' that have no direct English equivalents. Prepositions like à, de, en, dans, pour, par and sur overlap in meaning and follow rules that feel arbitrary. This workbook from the Practice Makes Perfect series gives these two problem areas the dedicated attention they need.
