About The Scarlet Letter

Nathaniel Hawthorne's The Scarlet Letter (1850) sets Hester Prynne in Puritan Boston, forced to wear a scarlet A for adultery while refusing to name her child's father. Reverend Dimmesdale's hidden guilt and Roger Chillingworth's revenge create a claustrophobic moral thriller.

Hawthorne probes public shame, religious hypocrisy and the cost of secrecy — themes that travel across centuries and cultures.
