About War and Peace

Leo Tolstoy's War and Peace (1869) interweaves five aristocratic families with Napoleon's 1812 invasion of Russia across more than 1,200 pages and 580 named characters. Battles, balls, philosophy and domestic tragedy share equal weight.

Tolstoy took six years and rewrote it repeatedly. Many critics call it the greatest novel ever written — daunting until you realise how readable the scenes are once you surrender to its scale.

About Leo Tolstoy

Leo Tolstoy (1828–1910), aristocrat turned moral radical, wrote War and Peace and Anna Karenina, then renounced copyright and embraced pacifist Christianity.
