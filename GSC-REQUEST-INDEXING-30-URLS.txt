--- SITEMAP (different tool — NOT URL Inspection) ---

sitemap.xml is NOT a page Google indexes in search results. It is a machine-readable
list of URLs for crawlers. "Request indexing" in URL Inspection only works for
HTML pages (articles, books, about, etc.).

To refresh your sitemap in Google:
1. GSC left menu → Indexing → Sitemaps (not URL Inspection)
2. Your sitemap should already show: https://www.lifewithbooks.co/sitemap.xml
3. Google re-reads it automatically every few days
4. Optional: remove and re-submit the same URL if "Last read" stays stuck on Jun 3

"URL is not on Google" for sitemap.xml is NORMAL — sitemaps are not search results.
