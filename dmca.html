DMCA / Copyright Policy

We respect authors and publishers. Here’s how to request removal of copyrighted material.

Last updated: 28 May 2026

LifeWithBooks does not host any books or PDF files on its own servers. All downloadable resources referenced on this site are publicly available on third-party platforms such as Google Drive or open educational repositories. When we are notified of a valid copyright claim, we promptly remove the relevant links and references.

Filing a DMCA Notice

If you are the copyright owner of a work or are authorised to act on behalf of a copyright owner, and you believe that material accessible through LifeWithBooks infringes your copyright, please send a written notice that includes all of the following:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorised representative.
  2. A clear identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (title, ISBN where applicable, author, publisher).
  3. The exact URL(s) on LifeWithBooks where the alleged infringement appears.
  4. Your full contact information: name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on their behalf.

Our Response

We acknowledge every valid notice within 3 working days. Once a complete notice is verified, the disputed link or reference is removed promptly, usually within 24–48 hours of verification. We will also notify the original uploader (where known) so they can submit a counter-notice if appropriate.

False Claims

Please note that under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages. Please only submit DMCA notices when you genuinely believe an infringement has occurred.

Personal Data & Privacy Removal

If a book or page reveals private or personal information about you that should not be public (for example, contact details, photos or sensitive data), please email us using the same address. We treat privacy-removal requests with the same priority as copyright claims.
