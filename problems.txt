Hard (serious — fix before AdSense or you risk rejection / complaints)
These can block AdSense, cause DMCA takedowns, or hurt your reputation.

#	Issue	Why it matters	What to do
H1
~20 language books use Google Drive PDFs (Longman, Cambridge-style titles, etc.)
Usually still copyrighted. Google and rights holders treat this as unauthorized distribution. Main AdSense risk on your site.
Remove pdf links OR replace with “summary only” + official buy link (Amazon, publisher). Do not host those PDFs on your domain unless you have permission.
H2
69 books show “Download” but have no PDF
Users click Download → “Being prepared.” Feels broken and low quality for AdSense reviewers.
Either add legal PDFs only, or hide Download and show “Overview only” / “Coming soon.”
H3
Site looks like a download farm (many PDFs, little unique writing)
AdSense wants original, helpful content, not only file links.
Add your own articles (how to learn English, book lists, author notes). Aim for real pages reviewers can read.
H4
Health books still send users to healthfruitstips.com
Not copyright theft, but leaves your site and looks like a thin affiliate/download site.
Host health PDFs on lifewithbooks.co (same as you did for novels) OR remove download and keep summaries only.
H5
4 books link to content.techsipweb.com
Third-party files you don’t control; unknown license. Same risk as random Drive links.
Remove or replace with your own hosted files or legal sources only.
H6
No clear license per book
Reviewers can’t tell public domain vs risky title.
Add on each book page: “Public domain” / “Summary only — buy official edition” / “Our original guide.”
H7
DMCA / copyright claims
Even one wrong book can get hosting or domain in trouble.
Keep DMCA page (you have it). Respond fast. Prevent by cleaning H1 and H5 first.
H8
AdSense “low value content”
Thin book pages = auto rejection.
Longer unique intros, FAQs, “who is this for,” reading time — written by you, not only copied blurbs.
Hard summary: Your biggest problem is not Gutenberg novels — it’s Drive + unknown external PDFs + 69 fake download buttons + thin content.

Cookie policy exists — add cookie consent banner when you enable AdSense.
N8
“Thousands of books” marketing text

N9

N10

OK for now; later use CDN or storage bucket if site grows.
N11
